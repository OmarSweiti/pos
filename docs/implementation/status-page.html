Jordan POS Build Plan

A verdict on the business master plan, the four compliance claims that turned out to be wrong, and the phased build order from a bare scaffold to a product someone else can sell.

Verified 20 Aug 2026 Tauri 2 · Rust · React JOD · Arabic-first · offline-first

The verdict

Part 1

Build on it. The master plan is materially better than the specifications most commercial POS products are built from. It gets right the four things cheap POS software gets wrong — and these four cannot be retrofitted.

  1. Sales are immutable facts; corrections are new documents. Eliminates the hardest class of sync conflict and the hardest class of audit dispute.
  2. Price and name are captured onto the sale line at sale time. Half of all historical-data bugs in POS systems are this rule missing.
  3. Stock is a ledger, not a column. Append-only rows merge across offline registers without a lock.
  4. Tax rates are data with effective dates. Jordan changes reduced rates by Cabinet decree; a hardcoded 16% is a re-release every time.

Add correct instincts on semi-integrated payments, on treating a card timeout as unknown rather than failed, on rasterising Arabic receipts instead of fighting printer codepages, and a rare willingness to enumerate 72 edge cases before writing code.

Four corrections

Part 2

Every load-bearing factual claim was checked against a primary or authoritative source. Four are wrong. One of them invalidates a phase gate.

C-1Blocks a gate
Plan claimed

JoFotara has separate sandbox and production credentials; Phase 2 exits when four sandbox documents clear.

Verified

There is no JoFotara sandbox. Testing requires credentials issued to a real ISTD-registered entity, where every submission is a live fiscal document.

The Phase-2 exit gate is unbuildable by anyone. Replaced with a 22-rule conformance harness, a fault-injecting mock ISTD, and five byte-stable golden documents. The real network hop became a gated Phase-5 certification milestone.

C-2High
Plan claimed

Order-level discounts must be prorated across lines.

Verified

ISTD rejects global discounts entirely. Discounts must be per-line and expressed as a percentage.

Proration is necessary but insufficient. The fiscal builder converts each line's absolute fils discount to a percentage, re-derives the amount from it, and asserts the round-trip. A mismatch dead-letters locally instead of failing at ISTD.

C-3Medium
Plan claimed

Round once per line to fils; the receipt summary is the exact sum of line taxes.

Verified

ISTD recomputes at nine decimal places and tolerates less than 0.001 JOD of drift.

The receipt rule stays — those fils are the money the customer paid. A pre-submit self-check recomputes at full precision and dead-letters any document that would drift, naming the offending line.

C-4Low
Plan claimed

Registration thresholds of roughly JOD 50,000 goods / 30,000 services.

Verified

JOD 75,000 goods · 30,000 services · 10,000 for special-tax goods.

The services figure was right; goods was low by 25,000, and the special-tax tier was missing entirely. A minimarket selling tobacco crosses at 10,000, not 75,000 — a very different conversation about whether the merchant must register at all.

A fifth finding changes the claim rather than the code: SAQ P2PE applies only if the terminal is in a PCI-listed validated P2PE solution. “Semi-integrated” and “P2PE-validated” are different properties. Without a listing number the merchant is on SAQ B-IP or C. Ask each acquirer for the number.

The build order

Part 4

Six phases, and they are a genuine sequence — each one's exit is the next one's floor. The test that matters at every gate is the same: what could a real store do?

0

Close-out

Complete1–2 days

Nothing. It compiles and it ships.

Workspace hygiene, CI, panic lints, product identity, two negative-tested guards.

1

Sellable MVP

8–12 weeks

Sell for cash, all day, fully offline, in Arabic, with correct GST and a printed receipt.

Money/Qty/ids/time · catalog + FTS · the tax engine · cart state machine · cash tenders · users, permissions, audit chain · Arabic raster receipts · atomic finalize · backup · sequences · stock ledger · RTL UI · seed fixture.

2

Money-grade

8–10 weeks

Take cards that reconcile to the fil, handle returns without being defrauded, close a shift that balances.

Payment terminal and the Unknown protocol · refunds, returns, exchanges · shifts, cash movements, X/Z · the fiscal pipeline · electronic journal · diagnostics.

3

Connected

8–10 weeks

Run more than one register, administer from a back office, keep customers under PDPL-grade consent.

Sync push/pull · chaos convergence · customers & loyalty · back office · device provisioning · licensing · telemetry.

4

Depth

8–10 weeks

Run three stores with promotions, receiving, counts, transfers, and a full report suite.

Price lists · receiving and WAC · counts · transfers · promotions engine · reports · shelf labels as a compliance feature · multi-store.

5

Harden & launch

6–10 weeks

Be sold to someone who is not you.

Year-one soak · Fiscal Certification · PDPL walkthrough · PCI SAQ with a QSA · restore drills · signing · staged updater · onboarding.

Roughly 40–52 weeks solo — a range, not a commitment. Phases 1 and 2 deliberately front-load the unforgiving parts, because hardware, money, offline and fiscal dictate the architecture. Dashboards never do.

Three things reality corrected during execution, each recorded in the phase file:

  • Enabling the panic lints immediately found three unwrap() calls on Mutex::lock() in production code. Replaced with poison-recovering locks, not silenced with an allow. The lint earned its place on day one.
  • The specified cycle guard, cargo modules --acyclic, cannot work — its graph is item-level, so any constructor returning Self reads as a cycle. Replaced with a module-graph checker that enforces the rule as written.
  • just dev-server never loaded apps/server/.env, so /health/db only ever returned ok if you exported DATABASE_URL by hand. Fixed at the source.
Both new guards were negative-tested — each was shown failing on a deliberately introduced fault before being trusted. A guard nobody has seen fail is a guard nobody should trust.

What “done” means

Part 6

Nine claims. Each is a passing test, a signed checklist, or a named advisor — never an intention.

  1. Sells offline.A cut cable changes nothing.
  2. Money is exact.No float touches it; every total is a property test.
  3. Tax is correct.The filing report reconciles to a hand-check on a scripted trading day.
  4. Arabic is right.Confirmed on paper by a native reader, defended by golden files.
  5. Cards never double-charge.No input sequence yields two tenders for one authorisation.
  6. Refunds cannot exceed what was sold.In any order, across any sequence of partial refunds.
  7. Registers converge.Byte-identical, through a chaos week.
  8. Fiscal clears.Eleven certification items, dated and signed — the only thing that makes “JoFotara compliant” true.
  9. Data is safe.Restored on real hardware by someone who did not write the code, twice, and timed.
