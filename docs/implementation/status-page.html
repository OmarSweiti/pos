Jordan POS Build Plan

A verdict on the business master plan, four compliance claims that turned out to be wrong — two of whose corrections turned out to be wrong in turn — and the phased build order from a bare scaffold to a product someone else can sell.

The verdict

Part 1

Build on it. The master plan is materially better than the specifications most commercial POS products are built from. It gets right the four things cheap POS software gets wrong — and these four cannot be retrofitted.

  1. Sales are immutable facts; corrections are new documents. Eliminates the hardest class of sync conflict and the hardest class of audit dispute.
  2. Price and name are captured onto the sale line at sale time. Half of all historical-data bugs in POS systems are this rule missing.
  3. Stock is a ledger, not a column. Append-only rows merge across offline registers without a lock.
  4. Tax rates are data with effective dates. Jordan changes reduced rates by Cabinet decree; a hardcoded 16% is a re-release every time.

Add correct instincts on semi-integrated payments, on treating a card timeout as unknown rather than failed, on rasterising Arabic receipts instead of fighting printer codepages, and a rare willingness to enumerate the edge cases before writing code — now 92 numbered ones, reconciled against the test suite by a script rather than by reading a table.

Build on it, but not from it. The two source plans under docs/plan/ are immutable historical inputs, and a superseded name in them reads as current truth: rate_bp, banker's rounding, stock_movement, tax_group, role_perm, a mutable loyalty_points column, down migrations. Master plan §4a is the ledger of every one.

Four corrections

Part 2

Every load-bearing factual claim was checked against a primary or authoritative source. Four are wrong. One invalidates a phase gate. A later independent audit then found that two of the four corrections were themselves wrong — both in the direction that matters, because each would have rejected fiscal documents whose arithmetic was correct. The current disposition of all four is master plan §4a.1; the cards below are the short form.

C-1Retained
Plan claimed

JoFotara has separate sandbox and production credentials; Phase 2 exits when four sandbox documents clear.

Verified

There is no JoFotara sandbox. Testing requires credentials issued to a real ISTD-registered entity, where every submission is a live fiscal document.

Conclusion retained, premise narrowed. The Phase-2 exit gate is still unbuildable and is still replaced by a 22-rule conformance harness, a fault-injecting mock ISTD, and five byte-stable goldens. But the specification is obtainable now: ISTD publicly lists its Technical Integration Guide, so pinning the official package moved out of Phase 5 and became microstep 2.7.0 — a precondition of every fiscal step. Nothing reconstructed is frozen before it, and no golden is signed off until it is pinned.

C-2Superseded
Plan claimed

Order-level discounts must be prorated across lines.

The correction was wrong

It said: convert each line's fils discount to a percentage and gate fiscal eligibility on an exact round-trip.

Do not implement it. A percentage is not a representation of the money, so the round-trip rejects baskets whose arithmetic is exactly right. Proration stays — largest-remainder, to exact line allowance amounts — plus a document recap equal to their sum, and the property is prop_document_allowance_recap_equals_sum_of_line_allowances. A percentage is provenance, never an eligibility gate. If one must be emitted at all, its precision is a single constant, open at 2.7.0.

C-3Superseded
Plan claimed

Round once per line to fils; the receipt summary is the exact sum of line taxes.

The correction was wrong

It asserted that ISTD recomputes at nine decimals and tolerates less than 0.001 JOD. That tolerance has no source.

Round-once-per-line stays — those fils are the money the customer paid. The local pre-submit check is redefined so it cannot reject an arithmetically correct document: a half-fil comparison per line, plus exact identities over the document's own carried values, never an unrounded invoice-level recomputation against a one-fil tolerance. A failure is QueueState::BuildFailed — neither an ISTD rejection nor an exhausted retry. The regulator's real tolerance is an open item owned by 2.7.0.

C-4Numbers kept
Plan claimed

Registration thresholds of roughly JOD 50,000 goods / 30,000 services.

Verified

JOD 75,000 goods · 30,000 services · 10,000 for a producer of special-tax goods.

Numbers retained, categories corrected. The 10,000 class is the producer or manufacturer of special-tax goods — a minimarket that merely resells tobacco does not enter it, which the first version of this card got wrong. Registration follows the merchant's registered activity and dated evidence, never their shelf assortment; GST registration and JoFotara obligation are independent axes.

A fifth finding changes the claim rather than the code: SAQ P2PE applies only if the terminal is in a PCI-listed validated P2PE solution. “Semi-integrated” and “P2PE-validated” are different properties. Without a listing number the merchant is on SAQ B-IP or C. Ask each acquirer for the number — and note that this is not only a wording change, which the first version of this finding claimed: B-IP carries eligibility and isolation requirements, and C pulls the store network, patching, access control, monitoring and policy evidence into scope. Which SAQ applies here is an open item; the acquirer's written responsibility matrix and a QSA settle it.

The build order

Part 4

Six phases, and they are a genuine sequence — each one's exit is the next one's floor. The test that matters at every gate is the same: what could a real store do?

Nothing. It compiles and it ships.

Roughly 53–72 weeks solo — a range, not a commitment, and up from an earlier 40–52. The old figure priced Phase 1 at 8–12 weeks for 87 microsteps in the only phase whose author is learning the language, then asserted that the hardest integration work “accelerates sharply”. The counts above are what the phase files actually contain. Record the real per-microstep cycle time over the first two groups and correct the range from evidence; an estimate nobody revises against measurement is a decoration. Nothing here prices the long-lead items — legal entity, the ISTD package, an acquirer terminal, printers, counsel — because those are queue time, not effort.

Phases 1 and 2 deliberately front-load the unforgiving parts, because hardware, money, offline and fiscal dictate the architecture. Dashboards never do.

Repository guards are maintained with negative tests: a guard nobody has seen fail is a guard nobody should trust.

Risk register

Part 5

What “done” means

Part 6

Nine claims. Each is a passing test, a signed checklist, or a named advisor — never an intention. A claim whose named test cannot hold is worse than no claim, because it gets weakened at the keyboard by the one person who can also edit the sentence; two of these were in that state and are restated here.

  1. Sells offline.A cut cable changes nothing — including fiscal: the sale completes and the document queues with icv IS NULL.
  2. Money is exact.No float touches it; every total is a property test.
  3. Tax is correct.The sales-side reconciliation by rate reconciles to a hand-check on a scripted trading day. It is not a completed GST return, and it is not described as one — purchases, input-tax deductibility, apportionment and box mapping arrive in Phase 4.
  4. Arabic is right.Confirmed on paper by a native reader, and defended by golden files a human can actually review — every raster golden ships a committed PNG beside it.
  5. Cards never double-charge.No input sequence yields two tenders for one authorisation.
  6. Refunds cannot exceed what was sold.In any order, across any sequence of partial refunds, against a link ledger that is itself a guarded fact table.
  7. Registers and the server converge.Three properties through a chaos week, over a specified canonical semantic dump — not a byte-identical one. The old single claim could never hold: facts travel up only, so two registers' fact sets are disjoint by design.
  8. Fiscal clears.The pinned official package (2.7.0) plus nine credentialed certification items, dated and signed, before any real store trades fiscally. Until every one is signed, the claim is “passes our conformance harness against the pinned specification” — and it is said in those words.
  9. Data is safe.Restored on real hardware by someone who did not write the code — including the case where the machine and the credential store are both gone and only the printed recovery code survives — twice, and timed.

The plan's own falsifiability rule, applied to itself: an edge case with no row is a gap, not an absence. Ninety-two numbered cases, each with exactly one of these statuses — and a script rather than a reader reconciles them against the test suite, the phase files, and the table's own arithmetic. The dangerous failure of a coverage matrix is not a red test; it is an absent test behind a green row.
